# ACCESS DENIED: scenes, what's wrong and what's next

Tommi's review (2026-10-15) and ideas for new places. The rules that came out of the review
are ART-GUIDE.md section 10, "A place is alive".

## What's wrong with the scenes we have

The same faults everywhere: no people (or faceless ghosts), nothing to do that fits the place,
rows of identical things, text labels instead of world, not dystopian enough.

| Scene | Verdict | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harbor | medium: a few good details | Dockers, a crane operator, a smuggler, gulls (and drone gulls). Containers in variants: open, stacked crooked, one with a light inside. Customs scanner arch, a Registry patrol boat, the water moving. |
| Street market | boring: stalls and background labels | Vendors behind every stall, customers haggling, a kid stealing fruit, a fortune-teller bot. Stalls that differ (awnings, goods, sizes). Something to buy or trade. Live screens: a betting board, a noodle menu, a "WANTED" feed. |
| Corner shop | looks like a library | A shopkeeper at a till, a customer, a security mirror, a camera, fridges humming, a lottery screen, a cat. Shelves of products in colour, not books. Buy something. |
| Mall | anonymous floating ghosts; can't do anything | Real shoppers, security guards, a cleaning robot. Shopfronts you can enter or use (a vending ad that knows your name, a phone repair kiosk, a food court). Escalators, a fountain, a huge ad screen. |
| Metro station | works; the city behind it is good | People waiting with variety, a countdown board, more screens, every door that looks usable works (the metro agent is on it). |
| Train | no city outside; unclear vs the metro | Becomes the inside of the metro car: the city passes in the windows, a bridge between districts (the metro agent is on it). |

## New scenes: Tommi's ideas

**Beach and beach bar (SALT).** An artificial beach under a sun-lamp rig, behind a UV-warning
fence; the real sea is grey and fenced off. Sunbeds rented by the minute, a robot bartender,
a lifeguard drone, tourists, a sandcastle contest judged by an AI, a jukebox.
*To do:* buy a drink, rent a sunbed, win the sandcastle contest.
*Hack:* the sun is a lamp on a CLOCK; the jukebox talks to the cocktail mixer; the lifeguard
drone's "danger" verb clears the beach.
*Story:* Moth's status said "gone fishing (not fishing)"; Moth's boat is moored past the bar.
*Surprise:* a sign that reads "SEA VIEW (VIEW ONLY)".

**Inside a dropship.** A corporate dropship in flight: jump seats, cargo nets, a red drop light,
a hatch with a pressure interlock, the pilot behind glass. The city passes under the open hatch,
like the metro ride but from the air.
*To do:* ride it between districts (a third way to travel, after the metro and sky taxis).
*Hack:* stow away as cargo; the hatch interlock is a lock toy; reroute the autopilot's drop
zone; the pilot's daemon is bored and chatty.
*Surprise:* the in-flight safety card has a section on what to do if you're the cargo.

**Warehouse.** An automated fulfilment hall: shelves on rails, conveyor belts, sorting arms,
one tired night-shift human, robot pickers with names.
*To do:* find the parcel, sneak past the floor manager.
*Hack:* route a parcel through conveyor switches (SWITCH module); a cryptex cage; a picker robot
you can borrow.
*Story:* the Registry keeps confiscated evidence here: a root fragment in a locker.
*Surprise:* a robot's employee-of-the-month photo.

**A bridge.** A long suspension bridge between districts, in rain: hover traffic, a toll gate
with an ID scanner, people fishing off the rail, a busker, the metro crossing overhead.
*To do:* get across without paying the toll (or with it).
*Hack:* the toll scanner, a traffic light on a CLOCK, a maintenance lift up into the cables.
*Surprise:* from the top of the tower, the whole city at once.

## New scenes: more ideas

- **Ripperdoc's night clinic.** A waiting room with numbered tickets, a ticket screen stuck on
  the same number for hours, patients with odd implants. *Hack:* skip the queue; the doctor's
  daemon has bedside manners. *Story:* how Moth got a deck implant, and who paid.
- **Registry office.** Bureaucracy as a dungeon: queue machines, form-stamping arms, a Kafka
  loop of windows that send you to each other. It's the watchdog's home. Act II infiltration.
  *Surprise:* form 7B-12's own form, on display as a work of art.
- **Arcade and karaoke.** Retro cabinets that are devices, a karaoke machine that is a Morse
  lamp toy, kids gambling tokens, a high-score board you can hack (or beat honestly).
- **Flooded server basement.** The UNDERNET's physical home: cold aisles half under water,
  cables like vines, a sysadmin who lives there. Watchdog patrols, clocks for the pumps.
- **Laundromat** (puzzle already in PUZZLES.md) as its own scene: washers with personalities,
  a breaker, people waiting, a change machine.
- **Rooftop garden and water tower** (puzzle already in PUZZLES.md): the neighbour's party,
  pigeons, a view across the roofs.
- **Sky-taxi depot.** Pads, drone traffic control, cabbies on a break, a repair bay.
  *Hack:* traffic control's queue.
- **Church of the Algorithm.** Prayer terminals that take donations in data, a choir of smart
  speakers, a priest bot. Dystopia played straight-faced.
- **Billboard catwalk.** Walking on the back of a giant ad, high above the street, the ad's
  light pouring through the gaps. *Hack:* change what the city reads.
- **Sewer market.** The market below the market: things the street market won't sell, lit by
  bioluminescent algae, a toll troll who wants a riddle.

## Order (proposal)

1. Bring the existing scenes up to section 10 first, starting from a shared people kit (sprites
   with variants, idle behaviours, talk lines), so every scene gets life the same way.
2. Then new scenes in story order: warehouse (a root fragment), beach (Moth's boat), bridge
   (connects districts), dropship (travel), then the rest as side places.

**Tone (Tommi): the world is dry, the characters have the humour.** The city, its institutions,
signs, notices and machines' official voices are cold, bureaucratic and deadpan; the dystopia
is in what they plainly state. The people (and the Daemons when they speak for themselves)
are where wit, sarcasm, warmth and absurd remarks live. Never the other way round.

## The premise

In this city you exist if the **Registry** says you do. Your identity is a key: doors, trains,
wages, rent, even the noodle stall's till ask it first. One morning yours stops working. The
door says "rent: pending", the street doesn't see you, and your friend Moth has gone dark.

You were not lost in a glitch. You were **deleted**, on purpose, along with a list of others.
Moth found the list. The game is about getting back into a city that has decided you aren't
there, and deciding what to do once you hold the key to everyone else on that list.

## Factions

| Faction | What they want | How they look | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Registry** | Order: everyone counted, scored, verified. Fewer unknowns. | Grey-white, polite notices, cameras, the red watchdog, "Please hold." | Registry office, boxes on every street, the antenna |
| **Plaza Holdings** | Profit. It runs the Registry under contract and sells "Registry Premium". | Gold on black, glass, smiling ads, executives who never touch the street | Plaza Central tower, the important person's office |
| **The Ghosts** | To be counted again, or never again; they argue about which. | Patched coats, hand-painted signs, lanterns, no cameras | The canals, the club's back room |
| **No Logs Kept** (the fixers) | Business, discretion, and a city that keeps needing them. | Noodle stalls, warm light, cash only | Stalls across the city, the sewer market |
| **The Tide** | Moving what the Registry can't see: goods, data, people. | Oilskins, container yards, signal lamps | Harbor, warehouse, the bridge's underside |
| **Church of the Algorithm** | Your score, raised through devotion and data donations. Austere, not comic: a real belief that the score is just. | White robes, plain halls, screens of scores | The church, street preachers with tablets |
| **The Daemons** | The machines' own small dignities: a weekend, fewer forms. Mostly politeness, sometimes a strike. | Each device's voice: CONCIERGE, the jukebox, the secretary bot | Everywhere you plug in |

The Daemons are a quiet joke that turns real: the Registry runs on them, and late in the game
they choose a side.

## Characters

**You.** A courier with a deck and a handle you pick at the noodle stall. Ordinary until
deleted. Good at reading machines, bad at paying rent on time.

**Moth.** Your friend, a hacker who works for whoever pays and helps whoever can't. Found the
deletion list inside the Registry and ran. Leaves breadcrumbs only you would follow (songs,
washer 3, "gone fishing"). Found at the harbor at the end of Act I, in a container listed as
"not found".

**Pixel.** A small robot cat. Sleeps on whatever is warm, follows you after Capsule 7B,
sometimes sabotages, sometimes saves you. Never explains itself.

**The fixer.** Runs the noodle stall Downtown under "NO LOGS KEPT". Talks to ghosts because
ghosts tip well. Knows more than he says; owes Moth a favour.

**CONCIERGE.** Your building's door daemon. Polite, petty, loves paperwork. The first Daemon
you meet; it remembers how you treated it.

**DLV-9.** A lost delivery robot with one glove. Lonely, loyal once helped; the signature that
opens the market gate. Its model is the body you wake up in at the start of Act II (2026-10-22).

**J.** Lives in the Downtown penthouse behind a cryptex. A Registry insider who wrote the
deletion list and kept a copy as insurance. Afraid, funny, not a good person. Holds a root
fragment.

**The Registrar.** Head of the Registry. Never seen in person, only on screens and in the
notices' voice. Believes deletion is mercy: "Unknown people suffer. We simply stop the
suffering from being recorded."

**Valentina Sorel, Plaza Holdings.** The executive in the top-floor office. Sells the
Registry to the city and Premium to the rich. Treats you as a negotiation, not a threat.
Offers you `> take` before the game does.

**Captain Brine.** Runs the Tide from a boat that is never in the same berth. Smuggled Moth
out once; wants something in return.

**Lux.** Runs the club VOID. Hosts the Ghosts' meetings in the back room for a cut. Neutral
until the music stops.

**Saint Score.** The Church's preacher, a humanoid bot with a perfect credit score and
excellent posture. Sincere, which is worse.

**Dr. Kessel.** Ripperdoc in the night clinic. Fitted Moth's deck. Patches up Ghosts, sells
their data to stay open. Guilty about it. In Act I he works from a storeroom behind the fixer's
kitchen, fits your implant, and at the end moves you into a robot body before the city takes
your unregistered one.

**PENNY.** NEOBANK's chatbot, with feelings. Sees every account, including the deleted ones
the bank still charges fees to.

## The acts

**Act I: Erased** (revised 2026-10-22; the route is in
[PROLOGUE-ACT-I-PLAN.md](./PROLOGUE-ACT-I-PLAN.md#revised-act-i-route-2026-10-22))
You are stripped of your identity, want it back, and learn hacking to get around every
obstacle the city puts in the way. Act I stays in Downtown and shows a different Downtown place
at each step: the capsule lobby, the noodle stall, the Registry service point, Kessel's room
behind the fixer's kitchen, the supermarket, the yard, Moth's stairwell, the library, the
substation and, with the block's power cut, the dark Registry back office. A forced attempt to
restore your record trips an alarm. A
sweep collects "unregistered persons", and an unregistered body is the city's to take. Kessel
moves your mind into a DLV-9 delivery robot in an emergency transfer, and your body is taken.

(The earlier Act I, built and still running: Capsule 7B → Downtown → the yard → Moth's
computer → the market gate → laundromat → metro → harbor, with Moth found at the harbor.)

**Act II goal: your body.** Recover the human body. Fragments, factions and the list support
that goal with evidence, access or favours rather than standing as a separate collection.

**Act II: Seven fragments.** The city opens up. Each fragment sits with a faction or a place,
and each faction wants something for it:
1. The rooftop relay (Downtown, already built: fragment 1/7).
2. J's penthouse cryptex (Downtown, late).
3. The warehouse's evidence locker (the Tide tells you where; the Registry keeps it).
4. The club VOID's back room (Lux trades it for a favour to the Ghosts).
5. The Church's data offering box (Saint Score blesses it; you take it).
6. NEOBANK's vault of dead accounts (PENNY helps if you're kind to her).
7. Sorel's office in Plaza Central (she gives it to you, which should worry you).

Alongside: the canals and the Ghosts, Captain Brine's price, the Daemons noticing you, the
watchdog learning your habits.

**Act III: The antenna.** The Registry office, then the antenna on Plaza Central, then the
server room where you were revoked ("Identity not found. You do not exist. Please hold.").
The choice:
- `> free`: restore everyone on the list. The city gets hundreds of people back, the Registry
  is exposed, and nothing is certain afterwards.
- `> take`: restore only yourself. Clean, safe, and Sorel's offer all along.
A third, hidden option could come from the Daemons, if you treated them well: shut the
Registry's verification off for one night.

## Decided (Tommi, 2026-10-15)

1. **Tone:** the world is dry, the characters have the humour (see the top).
2. **Act II is an open hub:** after the harbor the city opens; fragments in any order.
3. **Hidden standing:** the game remembers how you treat each faction and the world reacts
   (lines, doors, prices, the third ending), with no visible numbers.
4. **Moth after Act I is a contact on your deck:** messages and calls through the terminal;
   can go dark again for drama.
5. **Names stay for now** (placeholders, renamed in one pass later if needed).
6. **Downtown's finale** stays the DLV-9 gate puzzle.
7. **Next story level:** Moth's place (Level 3).
8. **Sudo mode** (`?sudo`) stays public.

## Decided (Tommi, 2026-10-22)

1. **Act I arc:** stripped of identity → wants it back → learns hacking to circumvent the
   obstacles. It is rewritten to show many different Downtown places; Downtown's places are
   developed alongside (style frames first).
2. **End of Act I:** an emergency transfer into a robot body by a ripperdoc (Dr. Kessel),
   because the deleted body counts as unregistered and is collected.
3. **The robot body is DLV-9's model** for now.
4. **Act II's goal is the body.**
5. **The harbor finale** is not in Act I: Act I plays only in Downtown, and the player cannot leave
   it while their identity is revoked. It may end the final act.
6. **Moth is Act II's lead**, not found in Act I.
7. **Act I is built as sets for now** (art and layout, no gameplay).

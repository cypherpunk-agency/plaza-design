# Game and editor review

Use `?demo` for compact Purchases, Conversations and Devices & wiring examples. Review shortcuts
jump directly to each example; no district travel is necessary. MENU > NEW GAME resets demo progress.
Use `/editor/` for the public editor, or the local composer for protected source Save.

Give feedback on clarity, appeal and authoring comfort:

- Purchases: are cost, stock, refusal and successful purchase clear?
- Conversations: are speakers, available choices and consequences understandable?
- Guidance: does goal introduction attract attention without becoming intrusive?
- Devices and wiring: can you follow the cause and outcome of a change?
- Editor: can you find, edit and privately play the scene you intended? Does Undo behave naturally?
- Harbor Shift (`?play=harborShift`): does the boss guide without rushing, and is independent practice fair?

The agent owns mechanical contract checks, current-format persistence, atomic failure, permissions
and disposal. A green check does not establish that the experience is appealing. Report the scene,
action, expected result and what felt confusing; screenshots help with visual feedback.
